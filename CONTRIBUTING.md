# Contributing

## Community Adapter Lab

### Deutsch

Für ein reales, anonymisiertes Adapter-Beispiel verwende:

**[Adapter-Beispiel einreichen](https://github.com/ftreppo/lrs-adapter/issues/new?template=adapter-example.yml)**

Bitte keine Namen, Diagnosen, PDP/Förderpläne, klinischen Berichte, privaten Nachrichten, Zugangsdaten oder private Drive-Links einreichen. Teile nur Material, das du weitergeben darfst; sonst ein synthetisches/anonymisiertes Äquivalent verwenden.

### Italiano

Per inviare un esempio reale e anonimizzato dell'Adapter usa:

**[Invia un esempio dell'Adapter](https://github.com/ftreppo/lrs-adapter/issues/new?template=adapter-example.yml)**

Non inserire nomi, diagnosi, PDP/Förderplan, referti clinici, messaggi privati, credenziali o link Drive privati. Condividi solo materiale che hai il diritto di condividere; altrimenti usa un equivalente sintetico/anonimizzato.

### English

To submit a real-world anonymized Adapter example, use:

**[Submit an Adapter Example](https://github.com/ftreppo/lrs-adapter/issues/new?template=adapter-example.yml)**

Do not submit names, diagnoses, PDP/Förderplan documents, clinical reports, private messages, credentials, or private Drive links. Only share material you have the right to share; otherwise use a synthetic/anonymized equivalent.

## Maintainer notes

Technical/configuration material may remain English-only.

Before changing the Adapter contract:
1. preserve provider/school neutrality;
2. add or update conformance tests;
3. record privacy/authority/assessment impact;
4. update CHANGELOG;
5. keep community submissions non-canonical until review/promotion.
